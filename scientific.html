Welcome to Polygon Analysis!

Learn how to analyze entire areas to find the best zones for your activities.

✏️

Step 1: Select Polygon Tool

Click the polygon icon in the map toolbar (top right) to start drawing your area.

📍

Step 2: Draw Your Area

Click multiple points on the map to create your polygon. Click the first point again to close it.

⚡

Step 3: Analyze

Select a date and click "Analyze with NASA Data" to get results for all points inside your polygon.

🎯

Step 4: View Results

See color-coded markers on the map: Green = Best zones, Red = Worst zones. Check the results panel for details!
